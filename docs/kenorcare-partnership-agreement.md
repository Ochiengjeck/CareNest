# Strategic Partnership and 50/50 Profit Share Agreement

**Date:** 2026-05-10

This Strategic Partnership and 50/50 Profit Share Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between:

**Party A:** `[Your Full Legal Name or Company Name]`, with an address at `[Address]` ("CareNest"), and  
**Party B:** **KenorCare** `[insert full legal entity name]`, a behavioral health clinic with an address at `[Address]` ("KenorCare").

CareNest and KenorCare may each be referred to as a "Party" and together as the "Parties."

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Agreement is to define the terms under which the Parties will collaborate to design, build, validate, launch, market, and commercialize the CareNest care and behavioral health management system (the "Platform") as equal commercial partners.

The Parties acknowledge that:

- CareNest will lead product strategy, software design, engineering, deployment, and technical operations.
- KenorCare will provide behavioral health domain expertise, workflow validation, pilot participation, clinical credibility, operational feedback, and marketing support.
- This Agreement establishes a **50/50 partnership in the net profit** generated by the Platform, beginning only after agreed development costs have been fully recovered, as set out in Section 4.
- This is a commercial profit-share arrangement, not an equity transfer, statutory partnership, or transfer of ownership in the Platform's intellectual property unless a separate written agreement expressly states otherwise.

## 2. Scope of Collaboration

The Parties will collaborate on the following workstreams:

- product requirements definition for care home and behavioral health workflows;
- review and validation of clinical, administrative, therapy, mentorship, and reporting features;
- testing and pilot use within a controlled operational environment approved by KenorCare;
- development of go-to-market messaging, case studies, demos, and sales materials;
- business development, lead generation, and commercial introductions;
- launch, commercialization, and customer growth of the Platform.

The Platform scope includes all nine functional domains: Public Website & CMS, User & Role Administration, Resident Management, Care Planning, Clinical Documentation, Staff & Scheduling, Therapy & Mentorship, Reporting & Exports, and AI-Assisted Capabilities. No domain is treated as a paid extra under either pricing model in Section 4.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

### 3.1 CareNest Responsibilities

CareNest shall:

1. Own and lead the software product roadmap, architecture, design, coding, testing, deployment, hosting decisions, maintenance, and release management.
2. Build and maintain the Platform, including core modules, reporting, role-based access, AI-assisted workflows, and customer-facing product improvements.
3. Manage technical documentation, developer operations, backups, and infrastructure arrangements.
4. Handle commercial packaging, customer technical support, and product licensing terms unless otherwise agreed in writing.
5. Implement reasonable security controls, access controls, audit capabilities, and data-handling safeguards appropriate to the Platform's intended healthcare use.
6. Keep accurate, transparent books and records of development costs, gross revenue, operational costs, and net profit calculations under this Agreement, and make quarterly statements available to KenorCare.

### 3.2 KenorCare Responsibilities

KenorCare shall:

1. Provide domain-specific insight on behavioral health, residential care, therapy, clinical documentation, treatment planning, compliance-sensitive workflows, and operational realities.
2. Review proposed features, forms, workflows, and reports and provide timely feedback reasonably necessary for safe and commercially useful product design.
3. Participate in discovery sessions, workflow reviews, feature validation meetings, and user acceptance testing (UAT).
4. Provide access to designated subject-matter experts, administrators, clinicians, and operational staff for structured feedback, subject to patient privacy and internal approvals.
5. Support pilot implementation planning and controlled product evaluation within KenorCare's environment as set out in the Timeline (Section 16).
6. Contribute to marketing and business development through market feedback, positioning insight, qualified introductions, approved case studies, testimonials, webinars, demos, conference appearances, co-branded materials, and value-proposition validation for behavioral health and residential treatment organizations.
7. Ensure that any clinical or operational guidance it provides is given in good faith and by appropriately qualified personnel.

### 3.3 Joint Responsibilities

The Parties shall:

1. Meet at least once per month during active development and commercialization periods.
2. Review roadmap priorities, pilot findings, market feedback, development-cost recovery status, and commercialization status.
3. Act in good faith and use commercially reasonable efforts to support the success of the Platform.
4. Escalate material legal, compliance, product safety, or reputational concerns promptly.
5. Jointly confirm, in writing, the development-cost baseline and the point at which development costs have been fully recovered (see Section 4).

## 4. Commercial Structure and the 50/50 Profit Split

### 4.1 Two Pricing Paths

The Platform is offered to customers under two pricing models, both covering the full Platform scope:

1. **Monthly Subscription — $400 per month per customer.** Low barrier to entry, no large upfront cost, hosting and infrastructure included, continuous updates and support, cancellable on 30 days' notice.
2. **One-Time Purchase — $10,000 (one-time, full ownership).** Full source-code ownership for the purchasing customer, no recurring platform fees to CareNest, customer controls its own hosting, payable on a milestone-based plan.

CareNest determines final pricing, discounting, and contract terms with third-party customers, but shall not change the headline models above for the KenorCare relationship without the Parties' written agreement.

### 4.2 Nature of the Split

The Parties' compensation under this Agreement is a **50/50 share of Net Profit** (as defined in Section 4.5), not equity ownership and not a percentage of gross revenue, unless the Parties later sign a separate equity agreement.

### 4.3 Development Costs Recovered First

Net Profit sharing begins **only after total agreed development costs have been fully recovered** from gross revenue. Neither Party receives a profit split until both Parties are made whole on development costs.

1. The current monetary investment to date, covering tooling and labour, is approximately **$3,000**. This is the baseline.
2. Additional development costs will be logged and tracked transparently, and agreed by both Parties as they are incurred.
3. Recovered development costs are repaid from gross revenue before any profit split.
4. Both Parties shall confirm in writing the point at which total development costs have been fully recovered.

### 4.4 The 50/50 Split

Once development costs are fully recovered:

- **50% of Net Profit to KenorCare**, and
- **50% of Net Profit to CareNest**,

calculated and paid quarterly. Both Parties share equally in every dollar of Net Profit earned thereafter, reflecting KenorCare's clinical credibility and CareNest's product contribution.

### 4.5 Net Profit Definition

"Net Profit" means gross cash revenue actually received from Platform customers, less the following:

- agreed development costs not yet recovered, until fully recovered (Section 4.3);
- operational costs of running the Platform, including third-party pass-through hosting, API, messaging, document generation, storage, and licensing costs;
- refunds, chargebacks, and bad debt write-offs;
- sales tax, VAT, withholding tax, or similar government-imposed transaction taxes;
- payment processor fees;
- approved reseller or referral commissions payable to unaffiliated third parties;
- travel or implementation expenses expressly passed through to the customer at cost.

Net Profit does not include, and gross revenue for this purpose excludes:

- bespoke one-off custom development that is not added to the core Platform, unless the Parties agree in writing that such work is shareable;
- grants, donations, or investment proceeds;
- revenue from unrelated products or services.

### 4.6 Payment Terms

1. Net Profit and the 50/50 split shall be calculated quarterly.
2. CareNest shall deliver a quarterly statement to KenorCare within 30 days after the end of each calendar quarter, showing gross revenue, operational costs, development-cost recovery status, and the resulting Net Profit and split.
3. Any amount due to a Party shall be paid within 15 days after delivery of the quarterly statement.
4. KenorCare may, at its own expense and not more than once per calendar year, request a reasonable audit of records directly relevant to development costs, operational costs, and Net Profit calculations upon at least 15 business days' written notice.

### 4.7 Performance Condition

If KenorCare materially stops providing the advisory and marketing support described in Section 3 for more than 60 days after written notice from CareNest, the Parties shall meet in good faith to renegotiate the forward profit split to reflect each Party's reduced or continued contribution. Accrued profit-share amounts already due remain payable, and the 50/50 split for customer accounts active at the time of the notice shall continue unless this Agreement is terminated under Section 13.

## 5. Intellectual Property

### 5.1 Ownership of Platform

All software code, product architecture, designs, database structures, documentation, AI prompts, reusable templates, branding created for the Platform, product improvements, derivative works, and related intellectual property in and to the Platform shall belong exclusively to CareNest, except for KenorCare materials that existed before this Agreement and except for any source-code ownership expressly transferred to a customer under the One-Time Purchase model (Section 4.1).

### 5.2 One-Time Purchase Customers

Where a customer buys the Platform under the One-Time Purchase model and receives source-code ownership, that transfer is a customer deliverable. The revenue from such a sale is shared between the Parties as Net Profit under Section 4, but the underlying transfer does not give KenorCare any ownership of CareNest's core Platform IP.

### 5.3 KenorCare Pre-Existing Materials

KenorCare retains ownership of its pre-existing materials, including its internal policies, forms, treatment approaches, training materials, brand assets, and proprietary operational know-how.

### 5.4 Limited License to Use Feedback

KenorCare grants CareNest a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, adapt, and incorporate feedback, suggestions, workflow recommendations, and non-patient-specific operational insights provided under this Agreement for purposes of building and improving the Platform.

### 5.5 Name and Brand Use

Neither Party may use the other Party's name, logo, trademarks, testimonials, or public statements without prior written approval, except as expressly approved for co-marketing under this Agreement.

## 6. Data Privacy, Security, and Compliance

1. The Parties acknowledge that healthcare-related implementations may involve protected or sensitive data.
2. If CareNest creates, receives, maintains, or transmits protected health information on behalf of KenorCare, the Parties shall execute a separate Business Associate Agreement before such access occurs.
3. Each Party shall comply with applicable privacy, security, and confidentiality laws and regulations relevant to its role.
4. The Parties shall limit access to sensitive information to the minimum reasonably necessary for the permitted purpose, except where law or an authorized disclosure permits otherwise.
5. KenorCare shall not provide real patient data to CareNest unless and until appropriate legal, contractual, and security arrangements are in place.
6. Any testimonials, endorsements, outcome claims, or public marketing statements shall be truthful, substantiated, and approved by both Parties before publication.

## 7. Product Governance and Decision-Making

1. CareNest shall have final decision-making authority on technical architecture, implementation methods, release timing, infrastructure, and product packaging.
2. KenorCare shall have advisory authority on clinical workflow relevance, behavioral health usability, operational fit, and market positioning.
3. If KenorCare identifies a material safety, compliance, or clinical-risk concern, CareNest shall review it in good faith before release.
4. Commercial pricing, discounting, and contract terms with third-party customers shall be determined by CareNest, consistent with the two pricing models in Section 4.1, unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing for a specific deal.

## 8. Marketing and Business Development

1. The Parties may collaborate on co-branded marketing materials, webinars, demos, outreach campaigns, and conference opportunities.
2. KenorCare's marketing role includes strategic positioning input, introductions, clinical credibility support, and participation in approved promotional activity.
3. CareNest shall own the final product messaging, website copy, demo environment, pricing pages, and sales collateral for the Platform.
4. Because profit is shared equally regardless of who sources a customer, the Parties shall nonetheless keep traceable written records (email, CRM entry, proposal record) of how each customer was sourced, for transparency and relationship management.

## 9. Confidentiality

1. Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public business, technical, commercial, and operational information received from the other Party.
2. Confidential information may be used only for purposes of this Agreement.
3. Confidential information shall not be disclosed to third parties except to personnel, contractors, accountants, or lawyers with a need to know and who are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations.
4. These confidentiality obligations shall survive termination of this Agreement for 5 years, except for trade secrets, which shall remain protected for so long as they qualify as trade secrets under applicable law.

## 10. Warranties and Disclaimers

1. Each Party represents that it has authority to enter into this Agreement.
2. KenorCare represents that it will provide domain guidance in good faith but does not guarantee regulatory sufficiency of the Platform for all jurisdictions or all customer use cases.
3. CareNest represents that it will use commercially reasonable efforts to build and maintain the Platform professionally and competently.
4. Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, the Platform and collaboration are provided on an "as is" and "as available" basis during development and pilot phases.

## 11. Indemnity

1. KenorCare shall indemnify CareNest against third-party claims arising from KenorCare's unlawful acts, unauthorized disclosures, misleading marketing statements made by KenorCare without approval, or violation of its obligations under this Agreement.
2. CareNest shall indemnify KenorCare against third-party claims arising from CareNest's unlawful acts, infringement caused by CareNest-created materials, or violation of its obligations under this Agreement.
3. The indemnified Party shall give prompt notice of any claim and allow the indemnifying Party reasonable control over the defense and settlement, subject to protection of the indemnified Party's interests.

## 12. Limitation of Liability

Except for fraud, willful misconduct, breach of confidentiality, data protection violations, or indemnity obligations, neither Party shall be liable to the other for indirect, incidental, special, punitive, or consequential damages.

Except for the excluded categories above, each Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total amount of Net Profit paid or payable to the Parties under this Agreement during the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

## 13. Term and Termination

1. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of 3 years unless earlier terminated in accordance with this Section.
2. The Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 1-year terms unless either Party gives at least 60 days' written notice of non-renewal.
3. Either Party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breach is not cured within 30 days after written notice.
4. Either Party may terminate immediately if the other Party becomes insolvent, ceases material operations, or commits fraud or willful misconduct related to this Agreement.
5. Upon termination, accrued payment obligations remain due; confidentiality, IP, limitation of liability, and dispute provisions survive; any development costs not yet recovered remain subject to the recovery mechanism in Section 4 from revenue of customer contracts signed before termination; and the 50/50 Net Profit split shall continue for customer contracts active and signed before termination for 12 months after termination, unless termination was caused by a Party's uncured material breach.

## 16. Timeline and Build Plan

The Parties intend to deliver the Platform over a focused four-month build, from contract signature to live deployment:

1. **Month 1 — Foundation:** authentication, roles and permissions, system settings, residents module, care plans.
2. **Month 2 — Clinical:** medications and administration, vitals tracking, incidents module, staff and scheduling.
3. **Month 3 — Therapy & AI:** therapy module, mentorship platform, AI integration, PDF/Word exports.
4. **Month 4 — Go-Live:** UAT and hardening, pilot inside KenorCare, production deployment, and the first revenue statement.

The first joint review occurs at the end of Month 1, and the first quarterly revenue statement is issued at the end of the Month 4 quarter. Timeline dates are good-faith targets and may be adjusted by written agreement of the Parties.
