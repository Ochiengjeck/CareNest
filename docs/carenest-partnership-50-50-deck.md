# CareNest — 50/50 Strategic Partnership Proposal

**Document Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2026-05-10
**Audience:** KenorCare leadership and CareNest Product Partner
**Companion Documents:** `docs/carenest-tor.md`, `docs/kenorcare-partnership-agreement.md`, `docs/carenest-engagement-models.md`

> This deck proposes a revised partnership structure at **50/50 Net Revenue share**, replacing the 10%/25% draft in the existing partnership agreement. It is deeper in scope, governance, and mutual commitment than the original draft. Each `## Slide N` section corresponds to one slide; the content is dense enough to double as speaker notes.

---

## Slide 1 — Title & Purpose

### CareNest — A True 50/50 Partnership

**Subtitle:** Equal share, equal stake, equal commitment.

**Date:** 2026-05-10

**Why this deck**

- The original draft (`kenorcare-partnership-agreement.md`) proposed a **10% baseline + 15% sourced bonus** structure, recognizing KenorCare as an advisor with marketing support.
- Both parties have indicated appetite for a deeper relationship — one where KenorCare is a **commercial co-principal**, not just a domain advisor.
- A **50/50 Net Revenue split** reflects that shift. This deck spells out what each side has to bring to make 50/50 fair, durable, and operationally workable.

**What's covered:** strategic rationale, the 50/50 mechanics, financial worked example, responsibilities, governance, IP, go-to-market, term & exit, risk allocation, and next steps.

## Slide 2 — Strategic Rationale: Why 50/50?

### The case for elevating the deal

| What KenorCare brings | What Product Partner brings |
| :---- | :---- |
| Behavioral health domain authority | Full software engineering capacity |
| Clinical credibility and regulatory know-how | Architecture, security, AI integration |
| Reference facility (operational proof point) | Hosting, ops, support infrastructure |
| Sales channel into behavioral health market | Commercial packaging and customer-facing operations |
| Case studies, testimonials, conference presence | Continuous product development |
| Active deal-sourcing and clinical sales involvement | Roadmap, releases, technical documentation |

### Why 50/50 (not 10/25)

- 10/25 fits an **advisor + marketing support** relationship.
- 50/50 fits a **commercial co-principal** relationship — KenorCare actively sells, refers, validates, and co-brands; Product Partner builds, runs, and supports.
- Aligned incentives: both parties earn equally only if the platform succeeds commercially.
- Cleaner narrative externally: "co-built by a behavioral health operator and a software partner" — strong story for prospects.

### What this is *not*

- Not an equity transfer. IP ownership stays with Product Partner (see Slide 11).
- Not a legal partnership in the statutory sense. The parties remain independent contractors.
- Not unconditional — both sides must meet performance commitments (Slide 15).

## Slide 3 — The 50/50 Structure at a Glance

### Headline: **Each party receives 50% of Net Revenue from the Platform**

No "sourced bonus" tier. No baseline-versus-sourced distinction. One clean number.

### Net Revenue (proposed definition — to replace Agreement §4.4)

**Net Revenue** = Gross cash revenue actually received from Platform customers, **less only**:

- Refunds, chargebacks, and bad debt write-offs
- Sales tax, VAT, withholding tax, or similar government-imposed transaction taxes
- Payment processor fees
- Third-party pass-through hosting, AI, messaging, document generation, storage, or licensing costs incurred specifically for customers
- Approved reseller or referral commissions payable to unaffiliated third parties
- Travel or implementation expenses expressly passed through to the customer at cost

### What stays out of Net Revenue (and is therefore not split)

- Bespoke one-off custom development not added to the core Platform (unless both parties agree in writing)
- Grants, donations, or investment proceeds
- Revenue from unrelated products or services
- Internal payroll, office, and overhead of either party (each party bears its own operating costs)

## Slide 4 — Worked Financial Example

### Assumptions

- Average customer: USD **$3,000 / month** (mix of Standard, Professional, Enterprise SaaS tiers).
- Pass-through costs (hosting, AI, processor fees) approximately **20% of gross**.
- Year-on-year customer growth driven by joint go-to-market.

### Projection

| Year | Customers | Gross ARR | Pass-Through (~20%) | Net Revenue | **KenorCare 50%** | **Product Partner 50%** |
| :---- | ----: | ----: | ----: | ----: | ----: | ----: |
| Year 1 (pilot + early commercial) | 3 | $108,000 | $21,600 | $86,400 | **$43,200** | **$43,200** |
| Year 2 | 10 | $360,000 | $72,000 | $288,000 | **$144,000** | **$144,000** |
| Year 3 | 25 | $900,000 | $180,000 | $720,000 | **$360,000** | **$360,000** |
| Year 5 (mature scale) | 60 | $2,160,000 | $432,000 | $1,728,000 | **$864,000** | **$864,000** |

### Read-through

- Year 1 is modest — pilot inside KenorCare and 2–3 early external customers.
- Year 3 is the inflection point — KenorCare's sales involvement and reference status drive customer acquisition.
- These figures are illustrative; actual outcomes depend on joint sales execution, market response, and pricing decisions (Slide 9 covers joint pricing authority).

## Slide 5 — Product Partner Responsibilities

### Engineering & Product

1. Own and lead the software product roadmap, architecture, design, coding, testing, deployment, hosting, maintenance, and release management.
2. Build and maintain the Platform — core modules, reporting, role-based access, AI workflows, customer-facing improvements.
3. Manage technical documentation, developer operations, backups, and infrastructure arrangements.
4. Implement reasonable security controls, access controls, audit capabilities, and data-handling safeguards appropriate to healthcare use.

### Commercial Operations

5. Run customer-facing operations: sales operations, onboarding, customer support, billing, renewals, churn management.
6. Maintain accurate books and records for revenue generated and revenue share calculations.
7. Provide quarterly revenue share statements and supporting documentation.
8. Own product packaging, technical support tiers, and SLA delivery.

### Joint contribution to commercial decisions

9. Bring commercial pricing proposals and material pricing changes to the Steering Committee (Slide 9) before implementation.

## Slide 6 — KenorCare Responsibilities (Expanded for 50/50)

At a 50/50 share, KenorCare's obligations expand beyond domain advisory. The deeper share is earned through active commercial and clinical contribution.

### Domain & Clinical (existing scope)

1. Provide behavioral health, residential care, therapy, clinical documentation, and treatment planning expertise.
2. Review features, forms, workflows, and reports; provide timely feedback.
3. Designate subject-matter experts, administrators, clinicians, and operational staff for structured feedback (subject to patient privacy).

### Reference Facility & Pilot

4. Serve as the **primary reference facility** — KenorCare's environment is the proof point used in sales conversations.
5. Allow approved prospective customers to take reference calls or limited facility tours.
6. Support a pilot implementation inside KenorCare's environment per a separately approved pilot plan.

### Active Commercial Contribution (new at 50/50)

7. Actively source customers through KenorCare's network of behavioral health operators, conferences, professional associations, and partner relationships.
8. Participate in **at least 2 sales activities per quarter**: webinars, conferences, prospect meetings, demos, or co-branded campaigns.
9. Produce approved case studies, testimonials, outcome stories, and co-branded marketing materials with reasonable cadence.
10. Provide clinical voice for sales conversations where prospects want to hear from a peer operator.

### Quality & Compliance Contribution

11. Contribute knowledge on accreditation requirements (e.g., CARF, Joint Commission) and regulatory expectations that shape product design.
12. Validate that delivered features remain clinically and operationally safe before general release.

## Slide 7 — Joint Responsibilities

The Parties shall:

1. Meet at least **monthly** during active development and commercialization periods (Steering Committee — Slide 9).
2. Review roadmap priorities, pilot findings, market feedback, sales pipeline, and commercialization status.
3. Act in good faith and use commercially reasonable effort to make the Platform successful.
4. Escalate material legal, compliance, product safety, or reputational concerns promptly.
5. Jointly approve external marketing claims, outcome statements, and co-branded materials before publication.
6. Jointly review and approve material pricing changes, new commercial bundles, and large enterprise discounts (>15% off list).
7. Maintain an agreed list of **KenorCare-Sourced Customers** in a shared CRM or written record. (Note: under 50/50 the share rate is the same regardless of source, but tracking is still useful for performance review under Slide 15.)

## Slide 8 — Governance: Decision Rights

### Steering Committee

- **Composition:** Equal seats — recommended 2 from each side, including one executive from each party.
- **Cadence:** Monthly during active phases; quarterly review meetings at minimum once the platform is mature.
- **Mandate:** Roadmap priorities, commercial strategy, pricing, marketing approvals, performance review.

### Decision Rights Matrix

| Decision | Authority |
| :---- | :---- |
| Technical architecture, infrastructure, stack | **Product Partner** (final) |
| Release timing and software engineering practice | **Product Partner** (final) |
| Customer support SLAs and support tooling | **Product Partner** (final) |
| Clinical workflow safety, clinical risk concerns | **KenorCare** (final veto on safety grounds) |
| Public clinical/outcome claims | **KenorCare** (final) |
| List pricing and standard packaging | **Joint approval** |
| Material pricing changes / enterprise discounts >15% | **Joint approval** |
| Marketing claims and co-branded materials | **Joint approval** |
| New target verticals beyond behavioral health | **Joint approval** |
| Customer contract terms (standard) | **Product Partner** (final) |
| Bespoke deals with non-standard terms | **Joint approval** |

### Deadlock resolution

If Joint Approval items deadlock for 30 days, the matter goes to executive discussion, then mediation (Agreement §15).

## Slide 9 — Pricing & Commercial Strategy

### Joint authority over pricing

- Standard list prices are set by joint approval and reviewed at least annually.
- Either party may propose changes; both must approve material changes (>10% list price movement, new bundles, new tiers).
- Discounting authority:
  - Up to 10% off list: Product Partner sales discretion.
  - 10–15%: Product Partner approval with KenorCare notification.
  - >15%: Joint approval required.

### Commercial bundles (proposed at launch)

| Bundle | Target | Indicative list price |
| :---- | :---- | ----: |
| Standard | Single facility, <50 residents | $2,500 / mo |
| Professional | Single facility, <150 residents | $3,500 / mo |
| Enterprise | Multi-facility / unlimited residents | $4,500+ / mo |
| Implementation Fee | One-time onboarding | $8,000 – $12,000 |

### Custom development outside the core Platform

- Quoted separately; revenue from bespoke custom work is **not** automatically split.
- Either party may propose that specific bespoke work be added to the core Platform — if both agree, the resulting revenue becomes part of Net Revenue.

## Slide 10 — Go-to-Market Plan

### Phase 1 — Pilot (Months 4–6)

- Pilot inside KenorCare's environment.
- Live workflows, real users, real feedback.
- Goal: validate clinical fit, operational reliability, and refine workflows before commercialization.

### Phase 2 — Early Commercial (Months 6–12)

- Conference presence (joint booths, speaking slots).
- Co-branded marketing campaigns.
- Reference customer program — KenorCare facilitates peer-to-peer prospect calls.
- Channel and partnership expansion (associations, EHR partners).

### Marketing assets jointly produced

- Case studies featuring KenorCare's deployment
- Outcome reports (subject to privacy and approval)
- Co-branded one-pagers, demo videos, webinar series
- Conference materials

## Slide 11 — Intellectual Property

### Platform IP

- All software code, architecture, designs, database structures, documentation, AI prompts, templates, branding created for the Platform, derivative works, and related IP remain **exclusively owned by Product Partner**.
- 50/50 refers to **commercial proceeds**, not ownership of the asset.

### KenorCare Pre-Existing Materials

- KenorCare retains ownership of its pre-existing internal policies, forms, treatment approaches, training materials, brand assets, and operational know-how.

### Limited license for feedback

- KenorCare grants Product Partner a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, adapt, and incorporate feedback, suggestions, workflow recommendations, and non-patient-specific operational insights for purposes of building and improving the Platform.

### Co-branding

- During the Term, the parties may describe themselves as strategic partners in approved marketing.
- Neither party may use the other's logo, trademarks, or testimonials outside the partnership without prior written approval.
- Use of KenorCare's name in case studies and prospect references is encouraged within approved boundaries.

## Slide 12 — Data Privacy, Security, and Compliance

1. Healthcare implementations may involve protected or sensitive data.
2. Before any access to protected health information, the parties shall execute a **Business Associate Agreement (BAA)**.
3. Each party complies with applicable privacy, security, and confidentiality laws relevant to its role.
4. Access to sensitive information is limited to the minimum reasonably necessary.
5. KenorCare shall not provide real patient data to Product Partner unless and until appropriate legal, contractual, and security arrangements (including BAA) are in place.
6. Testimonials, endorsements, outcome claims, and public marketing statements must be truthful, substantiated, and approved by both parties before publication.

## Slide 13 — Payment Cadence, Statements & Audit

### Settlement

- Revenue share calculated **quarterly**.
- Product Partner delivers a **quarterly statement** to KenorCare within **30 days** after the end of each calendar quarter.
- Any amount due to KenorCare paid within **15 days** after delivery of the quarterly statement.

### Statement contents

Each quarterly statement shall include:

- Gross revenue per customer
- Pass-through cost deductions
- Net Revenue calculation
- KenorCare's 50% share
- Aged receivables and any disputed amounts
- New customers and churn during the quarter

### Audit rights

- KenorCare may, **once per calendar year** at its own expense, request a reasonable audit of records directly relevant to revenue share calculations.
- 15 business days' written notice required.
- If audit identifies underpayment >5%, Product Partner bears the audit cost.

### Currency & banking

- All amounts in USD.
- Wire or ACH transfer to a KenorCare-designated account.

## Slide 14 — Performance Commitments

### Minimum commitments — KenorCare

To maintain the 50% share, KenorCare commits to:

- **At least 2 active sales contributions per quarter** (webinar, prospect call, conference, demo, co-marketing).
- **At least 1 case study or testimonial per year** subject to operational availability.
- **Designated SME availability** of at least 8 hours per month during active commercialization phases.
- Monthly Steering Committee attendance.

### Minimum commitments — Product Partner

To maintain the partnership in good standing:

- Quarterly statements delivered on schedule.
- Platform uptime SLA per published support terms.
- Roadmap deliverables agreed at Steering Committee delivered or rescheduled transparently.
- Customer support response within published SLAs.

### Cure mechanism

- Either party may issue written notice of material non-performance.
- The receiving party has **60 days to cure**.
- If uncured, the share structure may be adjusted (Slide 15) or the agreement may be terminated for material breach.

## Slide 15 — Underperformance & Share Adjustment

### What happens if commitments are missed

| Scenario | Consequence |
| :---- | :---- |
| KenorCare misses minimum commitments for **2 consecutive quarters**, uncured after 60-day notice | Share may be prospectively adjusted downward (e.g., to 35% or 25%) by mutual agreement, or this Agreement may revert to the original 10/25 structure |
| Product Partner misses delivery or SLA commitments uncured after 60-day notice | KenorCare may invoke termination for material breach (Agreement §13.3) |
| Either party becomes insolvent, ceases operations, or commits fraud | Immediate termination right |

### Why this matters

- 50/50 is generous on both sides. It works only if both sides keep showing up.
- The adjustment mechanism is a safety valve so that under-contribution doesn't become entrenched.
- The cure window prevents knee-jerk renegotiation over recoverable lapses.

## Slide 16 — Term, Renewal & Termination

### Term

- **Initial term: 3 years** from the Effective Date.
- **Auto-renews** for successive 1-year terms.
- **Non-renewal notice:** at least 60 days before the end of the then-current term.

### Termination rights

- Either party may terminate for material breach if uncured within 30 days after written notice.
- Either party may terminate immediately on insolvency, cessation of operations, or fraud/willful misconduct.
- Product Partner may adjust the share prospectively per Slide 15 if KenorCare ceases meaningful participation.

### Post-termination

- Accrued payment obligations remain due.
- Confidentiality, IP, limitation of liability, and dispute provisions survive.
- For active customer contracts signed before termination:
  - If termination is not caused by KenorCare's uncured material breach, KenorCare continues to receive **50% on those existing customer accounts for 24 months** after termination, then steps down to **25% for the following 12 months**, then ceases.
  - If termination is caused by KenorCare's uncured material breach, post-termination payments are limited to 12 months at 25%.

## Slide 17 — Risk Allocation

| Category | Risk | Bearer | Mitigation |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Build | Engineering cost overrun | Product Partner | Phased delivery, milestone reviews |
| Build | Scope creep | Both | Steering Committee change control |
| Operations | Hosting / AI outage | Product Partner | Redundancy, monitoring, SLAs |
| Commercial | Slow customer acquisition | **Both** | Joint go-to-market, KenorCare references, quarterly pipeline review |
| Clinical | Unsafe AI or workflow outputs | Both | Human review required, KenorCare clinical sign-off, scoped AI prompts |
| Legal | Privacy / HIPAA breach | Each for its own conduct | BAA, security controls, training |
| Financial | Underpayment or accounting error | Product Partner | Quarterly statements, annual audit right |
| Reputational | One party causes brand damage | Each for its own conduct | Approval gates on public claims |

### Liability

- Standard mutual indemnity for each party's own unlawful acts, IP infringement caused by that party's materials, and obligations under the Agreement.
- Liability cap: aggregate liability limited to the total amount paid or payable to KenorCare during the 12 months preceding the claim, except for fraud, willful misconduct, confidentiality breach, data protection violations, or indemnity obligations.

## Slide 19 — Comparison: 10/25 (Original) vs 50/50 (Proposed)

| Dimension | Original 10/25 | Proposed 50/50 |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Baseline share | 10% | 50% |
| Sourced bonus | +15% (= 25% total on sourced) | None — single 50% rate on all |
| KenorCare role | Advisor + marketing supporter | Commercial co-principal |
| Sales involvement | Introductions, approved promotion | Active sourcing, joint sales, 2+ activities per quarter |
| Reference status | Optional | Primary reference facility |
| Steering Committee | Monthly meetings | Monthly, with formal decision rights matrix |
| Pricing authority | Product Partner | Joint approval for list pricing and material changes |
| Co-branding | Permitted with approval | Encouraged, structured |
| Audit right | Once per year | Once per year (unchanged) |
| Performance condition | 60-day cure | 60-day cure + share-adjustment mechanism (Slide 15) |
| Post-termination tail | 12 months on sourced | 24 months at 50% + 12 months at 25%, conditional on cause |

### Bottom line

- 10/25 is the right structure if KenorCare wants a lighter, advisor-style involvement.
- 50/50 is the right structure if KenorCare wants to be a true commercial partner with material upside and matching obligations.
